## Audience-adjusted scoring v4

The deterministic v3 event-materiality model remains the base.

```text
base_score
  ↓
PRE-AI gate 45
  ↓
AI translation + summary + audience evaluation 1–10
  ↓
bonus 0…15
  ↓
final_score
  ↓
FINAL gate 60
```

Audience bonus:

- 1–5 → +0
- 6 → +3
- 7 → +6
- 8 → +9
- 9 → +12
- 10 → +15

The target reader is a practical AI-active professional: founder/owner, executive/manager, product/marketing/operations specialist, developer/technical specialist, or AI/technology decision-maker.

The AI is explicitly asked whether the story changes the reader's work, product, business, technology stack, economics, risk or available action.

## Why this is not «lowering the threshold»

The final publication gate remains 60. The 45 gate means only that a broader professional-news pool is eligible for an AI editorial pass.

A 45–59 item is published only if the AI editor judges it materially useful to the target audience strongly enough to reach 60 after the bounded bonus.

Scores 1–5 receive no bonus, so audience fit cannot rescue arbitrary noise.

## Image hardening

The publisher image path is now explicitly hardened:

```text
Google News wrapper
  → Google resolver
  → publisher URL
  → publisher metadata candidates
  → candidate validation
  → publisher Referer retry
  → Telegram sendPhoto
```

`intily_image_hardening.py` adds:

- publisher `Referer` on the first image request;
- no-Referer retry;
- explicit rejection of `news.google.com` and `*.googleusercontent.com` image hosts;
- reuse of the existing dimension/content-type validation.

The workflow activates this hardened fetcher before starting the production runner.

## Calibration rule
